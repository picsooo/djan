Tassili plateau trek, 8 days

The climb to the plateau with donkeys, the ancient cypresses of Tamaghit and the great frescoes of Sefar, Tin Tazarift and Idjabaren.

Trek with donkeysSefarRock art
Length8 days, 7 nights
LevelActive, on foot
SeasonOctober to April
GroupOn request
PricePrice on request

The itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Djanet and the Agba of Tafalalt

    Welcome at the airport by our team, then 4x4 transfer to the foot of the Agba of Tafalalt for the first camp under the Tassili cliffs.

  2. Day 2

    The climb, Tamaghit

    The trek begins with the climb alongside the donkeys, lunch at Tiffetest and night at Tamaghit, in the valley of the ancient cypresses.

  3. Day 3

    In Tinen and Tin Tazarift

    From Tamaghit to the paintings of In Tinen, discovery of the Tin Tazarift site and night in camp.

  4. Day 4

    White Sefar and Black Sefar

    Crossing the White Sefar and the Black Sefar to discover the famous rock paintings, then night in camp.

  5. Day 5

    Tin Kani and Alaghendemen

    From Tirassoutine to Tin Kani for lunch, then on to Alaghendemen for the camp.

  6. Day 6

    Idjabaren

    To the famous site of Idjabaren for lunch, discovery of the great rock frescoes and night in camp.

  7. Day 7

    Descent and the Weeping Cow

    Descent via the Agba of Aghoum, 4x4 back to Djanet to visit the market and a last camp at Tigherghert, near the Weeping Cow.

  8. Day 8

    Return

    Night transfer to Djanet airport for the flight home, with many beautiful memories.
