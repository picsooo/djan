Visa

Since 2023, foreign travellers visiting southern Algeria with a licensed Algerian travel agency can get their visa on arrival, without going through the consulate.

The request is prepared before you leave, from your passport and programme. The visa is issued at the airport for the length of the trip, up to 30 days. Fees are paid on the spot.

  • Passport valid at least six months after your return
  • Passport copy sent when booking
  • Programme and dates confirmed before the file is submitted

Source : Radio Algérienne

Getting to Djanet

Djanet has its own airport (code DJG). You reach it on a domestic flight from Algiers with Air Algérie.

During the Saharan season, a direct Paris-Djanet flight has operated in some years. We tell you which options are open for your dates.

  • Airport transfer included, at any hour
  • Night arrivals are common: the team waits for you
  • Allow time for the connection in Algiers

When to go

The season runs from October to April. The rest of the year, the heat makes travel difficult.

October to NovemberWarm days, mild nights. Beautiful light and the start of the season.
December to FebruaryPleasant days, cold nights, sometimes close to freezing. High season.
March to AprilTemperatures rise again. The last weeks before the heat.
May to SeptemberVery hot. We do not recommend travelling.

What to bring

Camping equipment is provided: mattress, tent, chair and table. Pack your things in a camping backpack (50 litres recommended). No suitcases, please.

  • Sleeping bag
  • Camping backpack, 50 litres recommended
  • Small daypack for hikes
  • Inflatable pillow (optional)
  • Insulated water bottle
  • Wet wipes and toilet paper
  • Head torch
  • Camera or phone
  • USB cable and power bank
  • Your medication and a toiletry bag
  • Hat or cap, sunglasses
  • Hiking shoes
  • Light clothes for the day
  • Warm clothes (fleece) and a warm jacket for the night
  • Sunscreen and lip balm

Money and network

The currency is the Algerian dinar. Most payments are in cash and ATMs are rare in Djanet: change money before you arrive.

There is phone coverage in Djanet. In the Tadrart and on the plateau there is none: tell your family before you head out.

Health and safety

Trips in the South are led by a local team who know the tracks. We recommend travel insurance covering assistance and repatriation.

Drink regularly, protect yourself from the sun and tell us about any treatment or allergy when booking.

Formalities can change. We confirm the rules in force when you book.
