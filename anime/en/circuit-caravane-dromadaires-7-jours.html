Camel caravan, 7 days

Five days at the pace of the caravan, from Adaik to the Essendilène guelta, then the Erg Admer dunes by 4x4.

Camel trekEssendilèneErg Admer
Length7 days, 7 nights
LevelModerate, on foot

PricePrice on request

The itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Tisras and Adaik

    From Djanet to Tisras, meeting the camel drivers and starting the walk at the pace of the caravan to Adaik, in a forest of stone and dunes.

  2. Day 2

    The Imourouden canyon

    From Adaik to the Imourouden canyon to discover rock paintings and engravings, then night in camp.

  3. Day 3

    The Tilalen valley

    Exploring the Tilalen valley, its sandstone pinnacles and the vegetation of the Tijlahikrar wadi, then night in camp.

  4. Day 4

    Tikoubaouine

    Across the high cliffs of the Tililine wadi to Tikoubaouine, with its arches, sandstone obelisks and pre-Islamic tomb.

  5. Day 5

    Essendilène

    Walk to the Essendilène canyon and its emerald guelta lined with oleanders. End of the camel trek and on by 4x4.

  6. Day 6

    The Erg Admer

    By 4x4 to the Erg Admer dunes, between golden sand, dark rock and blue sky, then night there.

  7. Day 7

    Djanet and the Weeping Cow

    The Weeping Cow at Tagharghart, the old town and Djanet market, then a last camp before the airport transfer.
