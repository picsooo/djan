Your questions before you go

Answers to the questions we are asked most often.

Sample content, editable

Before you leave

Do I need a visa to come to Djanet?

Yes. When travelling with a licensed Algerian agency, you can get it on arrival. The file is prepared before you leave. Details are on the Travel info page.

How do I get to Djanet?

By plane, on a domestic flight from Algiers. Our Tuareg friends meet you at Djanet airport. For the Red Tadrart tour, Algiers-Djanet-Algiers flights are included.

When is the best time?

From October to April. From December to February, days are pleasant and nights are cold. Each period is described in When to go.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as possible in high season, for flights and for the visa file. Send your request and Mina tells you the timing for your dates.

During the trip

Do I need to be fit?

Not for the 4x4 tours (Ihrir, Red Tadrart): the hikes are easy, often in the morning. The Tassili plateau trek and the camel caravan involve walking several hours a day. The level is shown on each tour.

Where do we sleep?

In a camp, in a tent or under the stars. Mattress, tent, chair and table are provided; bring your sleeping bag. See what to bring.

What do we eat?

Full board is included: breakfast, lunch, snack and dinner, cooked on site with the flavours of the Sahara. Tell us about any dietary needs when booking.

Is there network and electricity?

In Djanet, yes. In camp, no: bring a power bank. Phones can be charged in the vehicles.

Your group

Can we come with children?

Yes. Tours travel by 4x4 with easy hikes, and a tailor-made trip lets us adapt the pace for children.

Are the trips private?

Yes. Each departure is organised for you, whether you come alone, as a couple, with family or friends. Dates and pace are decided together.

How does booking work?

You fill in the booking request. Mina sends you a programme and a quote, then confirms the payment terms.

Another question?

Write to Mina, she answers personally.
