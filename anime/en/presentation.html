Passionate about Djanet

We are passionate about Djanet's incomparable beauty and cultural richness, and we devote ourselves to sharing its wonders with the whole world.

The journey

We guide you to the heart of the Algerian Sahara. Our tailor-made services let you explore breathtaking landscapes, immerse yourself in a rich culture and create memories that will last a lifetime.

The Tuareg team

Our Tuareg friends welcome you at the airport. Guides, drivers and cooks share the history, culture and natural wonders of the region, along with the authentic cuisine of the Sahara.

The real desert

A 4x4 expedition, hikes, rock paintings and engravings, nights under the stars in comfortable tents: our adventures promise both excitement and serenity.
