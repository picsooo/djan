Things to do in Djanet: 8 experiences in the Algerian Sahara

By Mina, November 10, 2026 Sample content, editable

In this article

Landscapes you should not missRock art and Tuareg cultureSleeping at a desert campHow to plan your stayPlan my trip

Djanet is the gateway to the Tassili n'Ajjer, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Here is what stays with our travellers.

Landscapes you should not miss

The red dunes of Tin Merzouga, in the heart of the Red Tadrart, change colour every hour. The Essendilène canyon hides a guelta between two walls, and the Ihrir valley a permanent lake in the middle of the desert, both stops on our Ihrir tour.

Rock art and Tuareg culture

The Tassili holds more than 15,000 paintings and engravings. The best known, the Crying Cow, is near Djanet. In the evening, tea, music and the stories of the Imouhagh guides are part of the journey.

Sleeping at a desert camp

A night under the stars is the strongest memory. Equipment is provided and the camp is set up by the team.

How to plan your stay

Allow at least four days on site. For an itinerary that fits your dates, send us your booking request or browse all our tours.
