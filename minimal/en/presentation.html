A meeting with Djanet that became a calling

Touring with Mina began with a crush: Mina's love for Djanet, its landscapes and the families who live there.

The story

Mina first came to Djanet as a traveller. She came back again and again, until it became her daily life: welcoming travellers from all over the world and letting them experience the desert the way she did, from the inside.

The Imouhagh team

Guides, drivers, cooks and musicians: the team is Tuareg and has known the Tassili since childhood. Mina calls them her family. They open the tracks, light the fire and tell the story of each place.

Our way of travelling

Small groups, a pace chosen with you, and respect for places and people. We leave nothing behind, and we take time to meet those who live here.

What we care about

We reply in French, English and Arabic.

Tailor-made

Every trip is built with you, around your dates, your pace and your group.

Support

Mina remains your contact, from the first question to the ride back to the airport.

Respect

For places, for Tuareg culture and for the families who host us.

Safety

Experienced drivers, maintained vehicles and routes we know well.
