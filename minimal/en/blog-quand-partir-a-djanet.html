When to go to Djanet: the best season for the Sahara

By Mina, November 3, 2026 Sample content, editable

In this article

The travel season in Djanet runs from October to April. The rest of the year, the heat makes days in the desert too demanding.

October and November: early season

Days are warm and nights are mild. A good time for the Red Tadrart and for families.

December to February: deep winter

Days are sunny and nights are cold: bring a warm jacket and a good sleeping bag. The end-of-year holidays are in high demand, so book early.

March and April: late season

Temperatures rise again and the light is superb. It is the last comfortable window for the Tassili trek.

Booking at the right time

Domestic flights to Djanet fill up fast in high season. Once your dates are set, send your booking request: we confirm availability within 24 hours.
